# Baseline Update Rules

Update durable baseline only when a change is:
1. intentional,
2. accepted/merged or otherwise established as production direction,
3. significant enough to influence future engineering/design decisions.

Do not update baseline from failed experiments, unapproved prototypes or one-off hacks.

After LEVEL_2/LEVEL_3 changes, baseline review is part of ACCEPT/INTEGRATE before closing the Work Item.
